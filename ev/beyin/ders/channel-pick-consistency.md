On 2026-11-03 the user rejected my 3 model channels:
- **Secret State Files:** its latest videos were only 2.6k–25k views against 62k subscribers, so it was falling.
- **What Exactly Is:** its median was 460 views; one 528k hit made it look good.
- **ChrizBob:** about one upload a month.

The mistake was picking by outlier ratio (best video ÷ channel median). A low median inflates that ratio, so the metric rewards dead channels that got lucky once. That is the very "tek kanal = şans" case our 5-gate recipe is meant to reject.

**Why:** we copy a format to produce many videos. The model channel has to prove the format pays on every video, now, and at a high upload rate.

**How to apply:**
- Agree with the user on the target criteria before searching.
- Judge a channel on:
  - the median of its last 10 long videos;
  - its trend (last 10 vs the 10 before);
  - its uploads per week;
  - how many of its videos clear a floor.
- Use the outlier ratio only as a secondary signal.
